Internal · Not for guests Prepared for Rancho Jaramillo

Working document, still open in places. Vendor and sponsorship figures below reflect the original 12 to 15 booth scope. See Budget Ledger for the scale note against the current 30 to 45 booth dashboard target.

Event Overview

Saturday, October 10, 2026, 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Rancho Jaramillo, 179 Enon Church Rd, Unionville, TN 37180. A private-collector car show on open pasture, gallery-walk pace, closer to a Concours d'Elegance than a swap meet. Show car target: 300 vehicles. Vendor village, awards ceremony, and on-site activities run alongside it. Marketing launch: August 30, 2026.

Run of Show

13 milestones from 6:30 AM traffic control on-site through 5:00 PM teardown, including the 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM mimosa bar and 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM Cítrico Grill service. Full timeline lives on the Ops hub's Run of Show card.

VIP Program

Two tiers, both standalone all-in tickets that include entry, so they can go on sale without waiting on the general admission decision. Both are built on the single covered VIP Area rather than a second structure. Hospitality only: signage, logo placement and naming rights stay with the sponsor tiers, so a VIP buyer never ends up as a cheap sponsor undercutting the title ask.

The tiers buy access, seating and shade. The B12 bar and the alcohol bar stay available in the VIP Area but are paid direct to the licensed provider and the bar vendor, never bundled into the ticket. That is deliberate on two fronts: it keeps a licensed medical service and alcohol sales out of a consumer ticket, and it keeps cost per guest roughly flat as headcount grows.

Ranch Pass

$400. Ten available. Event entry. All-day access to the covered, shaded VIP Area with seating. Reserved seating for Cítrico Grill service, 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM, with lunch included. Cigar lounge access. Luxury restroom trailers rather than the general units. Reserved parking near the gate, and a credential on arrival. The mimosa bar during the 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM window and the B12 bar are both open to Ranch Pass guests, paid direct.

Owner's Circle

$800. Ten available. Everything in Ranch Pass, plus early gate access at 8:00 AM to walk and photograph the field before general admission, a guided ranch tour, reserved seating at the 1:30 PM awards, a dedicated host for the day, field-adjacent parking or preferred display placement for anyone showing a car, and a merch bundle to take home. Merch proceeds go to Community Elementary School, so the gift carries the charity rather than diluting it.

How these are sold

Twenty VIP guests total against a 300-car field, so scarcity is the product as much as the amenities are. Lead with ten of each rather than the inclusion list: at these prices the line that sells is that there are only ten. Both tiers include entry, so if a general admission fee is introduced later it has to price under Ranch Pass, not over it. Owner's Circle at ten keeps early access and a dedicated host honest, which is what the tier is actually selling.

Activities & Attractions

Confirmed: live DJ & photo booth, a cigar lounge, chair massages, and a B12/vitamin shot bar (needs a licensed provider pouring it, not staff). VIP Area is a single covered, shaded destination built around those three amenities. Guided ranch tours are confirmed and run for Owner's Circle guests. Content & Photo Program: at least five specialty photo areas spread across the field, plus a branded Influencer Content Area step-and-repeat.

Still on the menu

A kids'/junior judge program, a pinstriping or detailing demo booth, a raffle or 50/50 tied to the charity split, a dealer/product reveal spot, and a long-shot mobile dyno experience pending a hard-surface site.

Car Show Categories & Awards

Best in Show, Best American Muscle, Best European, Best Import/Exotic, Best Restoration, Best Ranch-Ready, and People's Choice. Judging runs 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM with a 3-to-5-judge panel including the title sponsor. Awards at 1:30 PM keep winners on-site for photos and give the title sponsor a moment at the mic.

Food, Beverage & Merch

Cítrico Grill anchors the food program, 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM, open-pit Mexican fire cooking, real wood smoke and citrus brines. Whether Cítrico runs as a standard $250 vendor keeping its own sales, or is contracted directly, is still open. Merch, hats, tees, hoodies, tumblers, stickers, sells at two tables (gate and awards stage); 100% of proceeds go to Community Elementary School.

Vendor Program

$250 flat per booth, PaddockGavin's standard rate. Original scope target: 12 to 15 booths ($3,000 to $3,750). Every booth held to an experiential standard, a demo or hands-on activity, not a table with someone sitting behind it. Categories: automotive lifestyle & apparel, collector sneakers, detailing & care products, timepieces & fine jewelry, artisan & ranch goods, local food & beverage, real estate & luxury lifestyle, and non-selling car club/registry booths.

Tracked open item

The live console dashboard already reflects an approved larger scale, 30 to 45 booths against a $35K revenue target. This document hasn't been revised to that scale yet. See the Budget Ledger's scale note.

Sponsorship Program

Goal: up to two title sponsors, then open supporting tiers. Premier Title: presenting name in event title and marketing, largest signage placement, naming rights on one award category, VIP passes, and a speaking moment at awards. Secondary Title: second-position billing, signage, naming rights on a second award category, and VIP passes. Supporting Sponsor: uncapped, on-site signage, logo on materials, and a social mention. Vendor setup: $250+, 12-15 booths. Food and refreshment vendors additionally share 15-20% of sales with Rancho Jaramillo. One day, 9:00 AM-3:00 PM. Sponsor tier figures are held out of this file, which is publicly readable; they live in the proposal one-pager already sent to Rancho Jaramillo, pending their sign-off. The Community Elementary School partnership is worth working into the pitch itself.

Security, Traffic & Alcohol

Officers stationed at every entrance, not one flagger at the gate, rural county road with no traffic infrastructure of its own. Franklin PD's published extra-duty rate is used as a planning benchmark only: $60/hr per officer (3-hr min), $70/hr supervisor at 3+ officers, $10/officer admin fee. Unionville sits in Bedford County, outside Franklin's home jurisdiction, so the real quote needs to come from Bedford County Sheriff's Office or Unionville PD directly.

Alcohol service is mimosas only, 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM, poured by a licensed mobile bar vendor under its own Tennessee alcohol license and liquor liability insurance, naming PaddockGavin and Rancho Jaramillo as additional insured.

Health, Safety & Site Logistics

Restrooms via VannGo, a Middle TN family-owned company with a luxury climate-controlled trailer line plus ADA units, unit count still sizing against the 300-car crowd. Site infrastructure (picnic tables, canopies, a Quonset-style central structure) is rental, funding source open. Show cars park directly on the grass field; directional signage guides 300 cars into marque clusters starting at 7:30 AM staging, and doubles as a signage-sponsor opportunity. Cleanup/waste removal, general liability naming Rancho Jaramillo as additional insured, on-site medical/EMS, livestock boundary fencing (if applicable), and a connectivity/offline-payment plan for weak rural signal are all still open line items.

The "Pit Crew" Volunteer Program

50+ volunteers branded as the event's own Pit Crew. Roles: parking attendants guiding arrivals into marque clusters, and car-watch over the staged field through gates-close. Every volunteer gets a free shirt, hat, a TBD extra perk, and a photo with one of the show's most exotic cars. Every volunteer signs a waiver, same as show car entrants, before taking on parking or car-watch duty.

Charity Partner & Proceeds

Community Elementary School, Unionville, selected. The public elementary school serving the community the ranch sits in, so the money stays in the county the event runs in. Target: 30% to 50% of proceeds, and it is a target rather than a fixed commitment until it is locked. What counts as proceeds, gross takings or net after traffic control, entertainment and other production costs, is not defined yet, and the two produce very different numbers, so pin that down before the figure appears in any public claim or signed agreement. Merchandise sits outside that math entirely, 100% of Piston Powered Ranch merch sales goes to Community Elementary School, on top of the net split. Confirm the school's preferred recipient entity (school vs. district vs. PTO) before anything is published or signed.

Worth flagging

The console's own Event Info fact strip currently lists a different charity value. Worth reconciling which is correct for this event before either goes out publicly.

Roles, Responsibilities & Commercial Terms

Rancho Jaramillo provides venue access at no cost, load-in/load-out windows, parking area, and power/water (footprint and access still to be confirmed). PaddockGavin provides full event production and day-of staffing, marketing across owned channels, vendor and sponsor recruitment, judging and awards, traffic-control and mobile-bar coordination, and a post-event content report. The venue costs PaddockGavin nothing; production costs Rancho Jaramillo nothing. Vendor and sponsorship revenue is how PaddockGavin gets paid, the venue isn't taking a cut of either.

Open Items to Confirm

Grouped in the full document by category: Vendors, Dealers & Partners (outreach ownership, the Cítrico Grill arrangement, event-listing submissions); Alcohol, Security & Traffic (mobile bar vendor, traffic control provider and jurisdiction, insurance); Site, Infrastructure & Logistics (footprint, power/water, parking capacity, permits, restroom count, medical/EMS, livestock boundaries, connectivity, cleanup); VIP, Activities & Guest Experience (VIP Area build-out, the B12 provider); Volunteers & Staffing (recruitment source, the TBD perk); Financial, Sponsorship & Merch (pricing approval, signage sponsor, admission strategy, merch production); Charity Partner (event-partnership process with Community Elementary School); and Process & Coordination (a weekly Friday check-in cadence with Rancho Jaramillo and team).
