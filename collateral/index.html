Which file do I want?

PDFText or email itStandard 8.5 × 11. Opens on any phone, keeps the text sharp and selectable.
Print PDFTake it to FedExFull bleed at 8.75 × 11.25, so there is no white border once it is trimmed.
PNGDrop it in a chatA picture of the page. Previews inline in iMessage and posts straight to social.

At the FedEx Office counter: hand them the Print PDF files, ask for a trim to 8.5 × 11, and tell them not to scale or "fit to page". Fit-to-page shrinks the art and leaves the white edge the bleed exists to avoid.
