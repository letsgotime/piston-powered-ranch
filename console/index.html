Documents

Policies
& waivers

Draft template, not reviewed by an attorney. Structure and placeholder language only. Have Tennessee counsel review and finalize every document below before it's published or relied on for the event.

Privacy Policy

Draft outline: what's collected at intake (name, contact, vehicle/vendor/sponsor details, uploaded photos, video, voice notes, and documents), why it's collected (entry approval, event operations, day-of coordination), who it's shared with (Rancho Jaramillo, insurance, and public safety partners as required), and how long it's retained.

Needs before publishing

A real data-retention window, contact for data requests, and confirmation on whether any data is shared with partner brands or venue hosts.

Terms & Conditions

Draft outline: entry is by approval only and non-transferable, PaddockGavin reserves the right to decline or revoke any entry, event details (times, activities, layout) may change, and photography/video taken on-site may be used in future marketing.

Vendor Policy

Draft outline: $250 flat booth fee, load-in/load-out windows, what's provided (space, power availability TBD) vs. vendor-supplied, and code of conduct on-site.

Needs before publishing

Confirmed load-in time, power/water availability, and cancellation/refund terms.

Insurance Requirements

Draft outline: vendors and vehicle owners carry their own insurance; event liability insurance naming Rancho Jaramillo as additionally insured for the event date, per the existing Scope of Work.

Needs before publishing

Actual policy details and coverage minimums from PaddockGavin's insurer.

Liability Waiver

Draft outline: attendees, entrants, and vendors acknowledge participation is voluntary and assume the risks of attending an outdoor event on working ranch land; PaddockGavin, GoTime Motorsports, and Rancho Jaramillo are not responsible for injury, loss, or property damage occurring on-site, except where caused by gross negligence.

Needs before publishing

This is the highest-stakes document here. It needs a licensed attorney's language, not this placeholder, before anyone signs it.
